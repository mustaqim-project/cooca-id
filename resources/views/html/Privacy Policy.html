1. Our Privacy Commitments

COOCA is built on a foundation of data ownership...

  • No data selling: We have never sold customer data and will never do so.
  • No advertising profiles: We do not build advertising profiles from your business data.
  • No unauthorized access: COOCA staff access your data only with explicit permission or for urgent security/support purposes, with logged audit trails.
  • Full portability: You can export your data in standard formats at any time, at no charge.
  • Right to deletion: You can request permanent deletion of all your data at any time.
